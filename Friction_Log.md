## Afterward — five quick ones

1. **In your own words** (don't look back at the page): what's one move you learned?

2. Where were you **most confused**?

3. Where did you **almost quit** (or actually quit)?

4. Was there anywhere it made you **feel dumb** or talked over/under you?

5. What's the one part you'd **show a friend**?
