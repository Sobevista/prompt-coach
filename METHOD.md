# Method and evidence limits

Prompt Coach is a teaching hypothesis built from one practitioner's experience. It is not a controlled study, a psychometric instrument, or proof that every learner develops in the same way.

## Source material

The project owner reports that the original analysis covered 7,876 prompts from roughly 15 months of AI conversations. The recorded summary says average prompt length changed from 50 to 106 words and explicit output-shape requests changed from 0.6% to 8.9%.

The underlying corpus, sampling rules, calculation code and reviewable receipts are not included in this repository. Those figures are therefore owner-reported observations from one corpus, not independently reproducible evidence or general performance claims. Before quoting them publicly, the owner should confirm the calculation method and the right to use the source material for that purpose.

No raw conversation content should be added to this repository merely to substantiate a claim. A safe methodology note or aggregated, non-identifying calculation is enough.

## Validation so far

The persona rig uses simulated learner scenarios labelled with DISC, Kolbe and PI references. It tests whether the coach selects its intended diagnostic branch; it does not validate those personality frameworks, type a real person, or establish learning effectiveness.

The published v1 results do not certify the changed v1.1/v1.2-draft behavior. The stated validation gates remain:

1. Re-run the blind rig on the current prompt, including the enthusiastic high-volume false-positive case.
2. Give the trainer cold to a genuine beginner who does not think like Daniel.
3. Record what the learner could do afterward, where they became confused and whether the coaching felt respectful and useful.

Until those gates pass, describe Prompt Coach as a draft teaching tool, not a validated training method.
